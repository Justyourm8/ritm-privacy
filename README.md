# RITM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

RITM helps you keep personal records of finances, food, activity and time. The developer and data controller is **Pomazkin Aleksandr**. For privacy requests, contact **a.pomazkin@icloud.com**.

## Information we process

Your journal, notes, categories, goals and saved results are stored on your iPhone. Manual recording and searching the bundled food catalog do not send journal content to an AI provider or an external food catalog. RITM does not upload your whole journal as a cloud backup. Device backups and exports you choose are separate from RITM's server storage.

To operate your account and online features, our server processes account and device identifiers, authentication information, feature access and usage records, purchase or subscription verification information, and device name and app version when supplied. Connection and diagnostic information supports security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Apple handles Sign in with Apple and App Store payments; RITM does not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details from Apple.

Information is collected when you enter it, select material for a feature, enable an integration, use online services or contact support. It is used to provide the requested features, manage access and purchases, maintain security, answer support requests and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

## Optional AI processing and your permission

**OpenAI is RITM's external AI provider.** Selected information is sent through the RITM server to the **OpenAI API**, not to the consumer ChatGPT application. The information depends on the task:

- **Recording or structuring an entry:** selected text or a speech transcript, relevant purchase details, amounts or food components, language, currency, date, time zone, related draft references and your clarification. Structuring a note uses that note, its language and time, not all your notes.
- **Nutrition:** the dish description, ingredients or other details you provide, portion information, a selected photo and your clarification. A follow-up may use the material already submitted for that dish.
- **Daily reports:** selected daily indicators, food and category names, goals, reference values and calendar plans, comparisons with previous days or historical spending, and your clarification.
- **Period reports:** financial, nutrition and time aggregates, reference values, data-completeness indicators and your clarification for the selected period. This is not an upload of your complete journal.

A pseudonymous identifier may also accompany a request to prevent abuse; it is not a claim that the request is anonymous. Apple Health indicators are included in AI reports only if you separately permit their use for AI and confirm the request.

**Before sending a request, RITM identifies OpenAI, the selected data and the purpose, and asks you to choose Cancel or Send.** Canceling or dismissing the confirmation does not send that request. Permission covers that request, not future requests or your entire journal. Changed material or a new request requires confirmation; an automatic retry may reuse the unchanged material already authorized for that request. You can decline AI and continue manual recording and local catalog use.

Opening the camera or granting camera, photos, microphone, Calendar or Apple Health permission is not permission to send information to OpenAI. Local label recognition does not automatically upload an image when recognition fails. If you request AI interpretation of a food photo, the selected image is sent only after AI confirmation. Review photos and text before sending and avoid including unrelated people or sensitive information that is not needed for the task.

Voice input uses Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's systems depending on device capabilities and settings. An AI request uses the recognized text, not the original voice recording. Voice material associated with notes may remain on your device.

AI nutrition and other outputs can be inaccurate, particularly estimates from a food photo. Review results before saving or relying on them; they are not medical or financial advice.

## Permissions and integrations

With permission, Apple Health supplies steps, active energy and workout duration for your chosen features, not advertising. Calendar access supports the calendar features you enable. Camera, photo and microphone access supports selected capture features. You can change system permissions in iPhone settings and disable integrations or automations. Doing so stops the relevant future access but does not itself erase existing records or requests already sent. RITM does not delete original Apple Health or Calendar records.

## Storage and deletion periods

- **On your iPhone:** saved records remain until you remove them or erase RITM's local data. Copies in device backups, exports or other devices are managed separately.
- **AI content on the working RITM server:** request content and responses expire 24 hours after the original request. A clarification does not restart this period. Uploaded photos expire within one hour. Automated cleanup removes expired content; a technical failure can delay physical removal. Saved results on your iPhone are not erased by this server cleanup.
- **Accounts and service records:** these remain while needed to operate your account and are removed from the working database when account deletion is completed. A deletion-status receipt without the account or Apple identity reference remains for up to 30 days after completion. If deletion is interrupted, the operation and necessary processing records remain until it is completed or the issue is resolved.
- **Technical logs:** these may include request identifiers, endpoint paths, statuses, durations, errors and connection information. **No fixed maximum age is currently configured for all RITM technical logs.** Some logs rotate when they reach a size limit, but this does not establish deletion after a particular number of days. Logs may therefore remain longer than AI request content.
- **Backups and infrastructure copies:** copies may remain after removal from working storage. **A uniform maximum retention period for server backups and provider snapshots has not been established.** The 24-hour and one-hour limits above do not apply to these copies, technical logs or provider systems.
- **OpenAI:** its [API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) describe abuse-monitoring logs that may contain request content and ordinarily remain for up to 30 days, with longer retention possible for legal or safety reasons. Some API features maintain additional application state. RITM does not promise zero retention by OpenAI or extend its own shorter storage periods to OpenAI.

For a deletion request affecting support correspondence, logs or other identifiable records, contact us. We will assess what can be located and deleted, any technical limitations and any applicable legal retention obligation. This policy does not promise immediate erasure from every backup or an external provider's systems.

## Security

RITM uses HTTPS for server connections and encryption for AI request content, responses and temporary photos stored on its server. This is not end-to-end encryption: RITM's server and OpenAI must process the content for the requested AI feature. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may disclose information where lawfully required, including to respond to a valid legal request; this does not authorize unrelated use of your information.
