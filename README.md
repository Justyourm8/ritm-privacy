# RITM Privacy Policy

Draft revision: 4 October 2026. Not yet published; an effective date will be added when the policy is published.

RITM helps you keep records of finances, food, activity and time. This policy explains what stays on your iPhone, what is sent for processing and how you can manage your data.

The developer and data controller is **Pomazkin Aleksandr, Kazakhstan**. Contact: [a.pomazkin@icloud.com](mailto:a.pomazkin@icloud.com).

## Your journal on your device

Records, accounts, categories, goals, notes and saved summaries are stored on your iPhone. RITM does not upload your entire journal to its server as a cloud backup. Keeping records manually does not require sending their content to an AI service.

## Your account and smart actions

For sign-in, access to features and smart-action usage accounting, the server processes account and device identifiers, authentication information, and the device name and app version if provided. When you sign in with Apple, RITM does not receive your Apple Account password.

If you purchase paid features through the App Store, Apple handles the payment. Access and smart-action usage records are associated with your account but are not a copy of your financial journal.

## Smart RITM

Using Smart RITM is voluntary. By confirming that you want to send selected material, you authorize the necessary data to be sent to the RITM server and then to the **OpenAI API** to prepare records, calculations or personal summaries. This may involve a transfer outside Kazakhstan. Depending on the action, the data may include:

- entered or recognized text, a selected photo or document text;
- information about purchases, amounts, currencies, accounts, foods and portions;
- aggregate health and time indicators, goals and plans.

Summaries use aggregates for the selected period rather than a complete history of individual transactions. The current voice workflow sends recognized text to the server, not the original audio file. A clarification may include additional text and previously submitted context.

Permission applies to the selected request, not to your entire journal. Installing the app, signing in, reading this policy or saving a record locally does not constitute consent to send data to OpenAI. You may decline and keep records manually. Sending Apple Health indicators to AI requires separate permission in RITM settings.

Do not send passwords, access codes, full payment-card details or other people's information without a lawful basis. Remove unnecessary personal information before sending material.

Under the [OpenAI API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data), data is not used for training by default unless the customer separately opts in. OpenAI abuse-monitoring logs may contain requests and responses and may be retained for up to 30 days, or longer in circumstances described by the provider involving legal or security requirements. RITM's retention periods do not change OpenAI's retention periods.

Review results before saving them. AI can make mistakes and is not a substitute for medical advice or professional financial advice.

## System integrations

With your permission, Apple Health provides steps, active energy and workout duration. Health data is not used for advertising.

Calendar, camera, photos and microphone access is used for the features you choose, subject to iOS permissions. You can change these permissions in iPhone settings. Apple's speech recognition may involve processing on Apple's servers. Voice material associated with notes may be stored locally.

Wallet and payment-notification shortcuts pass the transaction information you configure, not your complete banking history. Disabling an integration does not delete records already saved.

## Technical data and external services

To prevent abuse, the server processes the connection's IP address. Technical logs contain request and service-operation information, including request identifiers, endpoint paths, statuses, durations and errors. This information is used for security and troubleshooting.

The RITM server is hosted in Almaty, Kazakhstan, by [Timeweb Cloud](https://timeweb.cloud/personal-data-apps). [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) provides sign-in, system integrations and purchases; [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) provides AI processing. Apple and OpenAI may process data in other countries.

When you request product information using an external link, the server may contact the corresponding website. If this policy is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), GitHub receives technical information about visits to the page, not your RITM journal.

## Retention and security

- **On your iPhone:** records and summaries remain until you delete them using the app or your device.
- **AI requests on the RITM server:** request content and responses are accessible for up to 24 hours from creation of the original request. Clarifications do not extend this period.
- **Images on the server:** images are accessible for no more than one hour after upload. Extracted text may remain accessible within the request's retention period.
- **Account and associated service records:** these are retained while the account exists and removed from the working database when account deletion is completed. The 24-hour period does not apply to these records. A de-identified receipt of completed deletion is retained for up to 30 days.

When the retention period expires, access to AI-request content stops. Automated cleanup removes it from the working database and storage without a separate request. A failure may delay physical cleanup. Results saved on your iPhone remain there.

Monthly server backups and technical logs are separate from live AI-request storage. The 24-hour and one-hour limits do not promise erasure of every copy. Deletion from the working database does not mean immediate deletion from backups or external providers.

Connections are protected by HTTPS. AI-request content, responses and temporary images are encrypted at rest on the server. This is not end-to-end encryption: the server and OpenAI process the content. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising tracking across apps and websites. Information may be disclosed to the extent necessary to meet a legal obligation.

## Deletion and your choices

In settings, **“Erase data on this iPhone”** deletes local records, not the server account. **“Delete account”** initiates deletion of the account's server data and RITM records on this iPhone. Signing in again with Apple may be required. Signing out does not delete data.

Copies on other devices and exported files must be deleted separately. RITM does not delete original Apple Health or Calendar records. Apple subscriptions must be cancelled separately.

To request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent, email [a.pomazkin@icloud.com](mailto:a.pomazkin@icloud.com). We may need to verify account ownership, but we will not ask for your password. Declining AI processing stops new submissions; it does not erase material already sent. Requests concerning processing and deletion are handled subject to applicable law and retention obligations.

Consent does not waive mandatory user rights or RITM's obligations under applicable law.
